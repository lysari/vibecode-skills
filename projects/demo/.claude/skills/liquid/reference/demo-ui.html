Press

Hold one down. It flattens and widens to 1.08 0.9 in 110ms and stays there; let go and it springs back through 0.95 1.07 over 3.4× as long. Keyboard too — tab to one and hold Enter.

Fold

An item entering or leaving a row. Size, padding and margin fold together, so the row closes the gap as it goes — and the content beads up on the same clock, swelling to 1.14 as it gathers. Add a few, then remove them mid-fold.

Grow from origin

The panel comes out of the button, not over it: 340ms open on a spring that overshoots, 200ms tighter close, and the content one 40ms lag behind the box out of blur(12px). Escape or a click outside closes it.

Panel

Slide — travelling

One surface moving past a window. The panels either side stay on screen at scale(0.94), half dimmed and 3px blurred; the frame's height follows on its own shorter clock. Click through fast — the travel picks up the live position rather than restarting.

First

The track travels and the neighbours stay visible, so the switch reads as a place you are moving through.

Second

Deliberately a taller panel, so the frame has a height change to follow. The resize is 280ms against the travel's 420ms — it has stopped before the content lands, rather than still growing under it. If both ran on one clock the frame would look like it was being dragged along by the content.

Third

Short again, so the frame has to come back down.

Slide — swapping

The same content change in one spot: one curve and one duration both ways, so the two panels always cover the frame between them. Drift is 6px and the blur 3px — both are paid twice, once by each half.

Overview

Crossing over in place. Watch the middle of the cross: the frame is never allowed to show through.

Detail

Fading the old one out over a shorter window than the new one fades in would let the page through in the middle, and across a whole section that dip reads as a flash rather than as a change of content.

Settings

Same shape in both directions, so going back looks like going forward reversed.
