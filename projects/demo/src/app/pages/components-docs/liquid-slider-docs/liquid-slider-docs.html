Liquid slider

A number picked by dragging across the field, with its label and value written inside it. The fill follows the finger on a spring, settles onto the nearest step when it lets go, and stretches the field a little when dragged past either end. Click to jump, or focus it and use the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End.

Sizes

Everything inside the field is sized off --liquid-slider-height: the padding, the handle, the dots, the gap and the default font. A rounder --liquid-slider-radius gives the fill a rounder end, up to a circle for a pill. --liquid-slider-gap insets the fill on every side, with corners that stay concentric with the field's.
