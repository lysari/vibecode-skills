Liquid menu popup

A panel that grows out of the element that opened it and shrinks back into it. It places itself under its trigger, flips above when only that side fits, and stacks on top of another popup when opened from inside one.

Closing

By default an invisible overlay covers the page while the popup is up, so the click outside closes it and goes no further. Turn overlay off and the page stays live: the click outside still closes the popup, and also lands on whatever it hit. Turn closeOnOutsideClick off and a click outside leaves it up — Escape, the trigger, close() or focus moving out of the panel still put it away.

Click outside, press Escape, or use the button.
