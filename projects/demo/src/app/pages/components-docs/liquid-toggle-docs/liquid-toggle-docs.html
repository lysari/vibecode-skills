Liquid toggle

A boolean chosen by sliding a thumb across a pill. The thumb pours to the other end and the on-color fills the track from the left edge. fill="thumb" grows that color out from behind the thumb instead. io adds an I on the color and an O on the empty track. Click the track, its label, or focus it and press Space.
