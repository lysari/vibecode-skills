Liquid upload

A single-file field, drawn like the form's other inputs: the file's name inside a field of the same height, and a Browse button at its end. A file can also be dropped onto it. The value is an IFile carrying a small preview; putting the original somewhere is the caller's job, through picked.

Photo

variant="photo" shows the photo itself in a round avatar beside the field. minWidth and minHeight are checked against an image before it is taken. Anything projected inside sits under the field — the rules the photo has to meet.

  • At least 400 × 500 pixels
  • Plain white background, face centred

Rules

A file is checked against accept, maxSize and, for an image, its minimum size — however it arrived, picked or dropped. One that fails leaves the value as it was, says why under the field, and is reported through rejected. Try an image here, or a PDF over 200 KB.
